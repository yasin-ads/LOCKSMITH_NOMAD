What to Do When Locked Out

A lockout is stressful, but a few safe checks can prevent damage.

What you should know

Check other authorized entrances, confirm whether a trusted person has a spare, and avoid forcing the door or attempting unsafe entry.

When to contact a professional

For urgent safety concerns, contact the appropriate emergency service. Otherwise, call a locksmith and describe the lock and location.
