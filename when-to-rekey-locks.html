When Should You Rekey Your Locks?

Rekeying changes which key operates an existing lock without necessarily replacing the entire lock.

What you should know

Consider rekeying after moving into a new property, losing a key, changing tenants, or when you cannot account for every copy.

When to contact a professional

A technician can inspect the lock cylinder and advise whether rekeying or replacing the hardware is more suitable.
